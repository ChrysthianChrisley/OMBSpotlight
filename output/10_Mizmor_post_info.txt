#10 - Mizmor | One-Man Band Spotlight #Shorts


=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Founded in 2012 in Portland, Oregon, Mizmor (מזמור) stands as a towering monument of blackened doom and existential dread in modern extreme music.

Behind the suffocating sonic weight is solitary mastermind A.L.N. Operating in complete isolation, he wrote, performed, recorded, and mixed every instrument and vocal line himself on his monumental 2016 masterpiece "Yodh".

Adorned with the haunting artwork of Zdzisław Beksiński, "Yodh" remains one of the most intense and cathartic artistic statements in underground metal history.
