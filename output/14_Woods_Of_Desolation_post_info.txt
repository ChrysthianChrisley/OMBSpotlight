Founded in 2005 in Australia, Woods of Desolation stands as one of the most revered and influential post-black metal / DSBM entities in extreme music.

Behind every evocative melody and driving rhythm is sole mastermind and composer D. (also known as Dolor). Writing, arranging, and tracking every guitar and bass layer in solitude, he created the 2011 masterpiece "Torn Beyond Reason".
