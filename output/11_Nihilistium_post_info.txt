=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Emerging from the United Kingdom, Nihilistium stands as one of the most raw, agonizing, and poignant solo DSBM projects in modern underground black metal.

Behind this suffocating sonic creation is sole musician Mörtemiis. Operating in total solitude, Mörtemiis performs every howling vocal, bleak guitar chord, and melancholic rhythm, crafting an intimate journey into absolute despair on the self-titled album.
