Founded in 1998 in San Francisco, California, Leviathan became the definitive monument of American black metal. 

Behind every note was a solitary tortured visionary: Jef Whitehead, known as Wrest. Operating in complete isolation, he wrote, performed, and recorded every instrument and vocal line himself on a four-track analog recorder, releasing the seminal black metal masterpiece "The Tenth Sub Level of Suicide" in 2003.

Wrest's solitary artistic vision and genuine misanthropic despair poured into sound remain an enduring monument in extreme music history.
