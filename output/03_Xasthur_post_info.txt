Emerging from Alhambra, California, Xasthur stands as the bleakest embodiment of suicidal and depressive black metal.

Behind every ghostly layer was one reclusive musician: Scott Conner, known as Malefic. Working in complete solitude, he wrote, performed, and recorded every instrument and tortured vocal performance himself, releasing his harrowing second album "The Funeral of Being" in 2003.

Malefic's solitary artistic vision and haunting, spectral misery poured into sound remain an enduring monument in extreme music history.
