=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Founded in 2007 in Louisville, Kentucky, Panopticon redefined atmospheric black metal by weaving extreme fury with American folk and bluegrass roots.

Behind every note was multi-instrumentalist Austin Lunn (A. Lunn). Performing drums, guitars, bass, flutes, banjos, and vocals alone in personal immersion, he released the widely acclaimed masterpiece "Autumn Eternal" in 2015.

Austin Lunn's passionate solitary vision remains an enduring monument to natural sorrow, memory, and artistic independence.
