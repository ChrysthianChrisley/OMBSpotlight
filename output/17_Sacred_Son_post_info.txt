Emerging from the United Kingdom, Sacred Son gained international attention with its unconventional holiday beach album cover, contrasting sharply with its deeply evocative and epic atmospheric black metal sound.

Behind the debut album was solitary musician Dane Cross. Operating in complete creative autonomy, Cross wrote, performed, programmed, and recorded every instrument and vocal line one hundred percent alone.
