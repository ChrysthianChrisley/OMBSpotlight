Founded in 1996 in Hesse, Germany, Bilskirnir stands as one of the rawest and most enduring solo projects in underground pagan black metal.

Behind every note was multi-instrumentalist Widar. Operating in complete autonomy, he wrote, performed, and recorded every instrument himself, releasing the cult masterpiece "Wotansvolk" in 2007.

Widar's unyielding solitary vision and archaic European atmosphere remain an influential monument in underground extreme metal.
