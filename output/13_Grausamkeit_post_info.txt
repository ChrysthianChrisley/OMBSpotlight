=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Formed in 1994 in Germany, Grausamkeit is the notorious and iconic lo-fi raw black metal project forged by sole mastermind B.S.o.D. (Andreas Bettinger).

Operating in complete bedroom isolation, Bettinger recorded every demented guitar riff, screeching synth line, and abrasive vocal scream alone onto tape, creating the 1999 underground cult classic "Stardust".

🎧 Featured tracks in this video:
01. PCP Jesus
02. Stardust (Midtro "Jill's Death")
