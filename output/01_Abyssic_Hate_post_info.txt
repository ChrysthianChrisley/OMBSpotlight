=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Founded in 1993 in Australia, Abyssic Hate stands as one of the darkest and most influential one-man band projects in black metal history. 

Behind every note was a solitary mastermind: Shane Rout. Operating in complete isolation, he wrote, performed, and recorded every instrument and vocal line himself, releasing the seminal depressive black metal masterpiece "Suicidal Emotions" in 2000.

Tragically, in March of 2026, the underground metal community lost Shane Rout at the age of 53. His solitary artistic vision and genuine sorrow poured into sound remain an enduring monument in extreme music history. Rest in peace.
