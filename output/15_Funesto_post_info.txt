=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Hailing from Brazil, Funesto stands as one of the rawest and most agonizing solitary DSBM projects in South American extreme metal history.

Behind every harrowing scream, lo-fi tremolo riff, and hypnotic drum beat was sole mastermind Funebre (Sergio França). Operating in complete isolation, he channeled pure psychological grief and desolate despair into the 2007 cult masterpiece "Depressivos hinos suicidas".

02. Meus Gritos Não São Ouvidos
