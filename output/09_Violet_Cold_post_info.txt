=== DESCRIÇÃO (Copiar e colar) ===
Founded in 2013 in Baku, Azerbaijan, Violet Cold stands as one of the most visionary and genre-defying solo projects in contemporary extreme music.

Behind every note was sole multi-instrumentalist Emin Guliyev. Operating in complete independence, he wrote, performed, recorded, and produced his monumental 2017 blackgaze masterpiece "Anomie".

Emin Guliyev's breathtaking fusion of euphoric post-rock, cosmic electronics, and searing black metal remains a landmark in modern metal history.
