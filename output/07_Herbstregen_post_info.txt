Founded in Germany, Herbstregen emerged as one of the rawest and most sincere expressions of depressive black metal.

Behind every note was solitary creator Herbst (also known as Inferno). Composing, recording, and screaming in total isolation, he created the cult DSBM opus "Leben Gescheitert" in 2007.

Herbstregen's piercing mournful riffs and vulnerable acoustic melancholia remain an enduring underground testament to solitary sorrow.
