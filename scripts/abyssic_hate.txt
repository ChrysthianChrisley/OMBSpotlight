Founded in 1993 in Australia, Abyssic Hate stands as one of the darkest and most influential one-man band projects in the history of underground black metal.
Behind every note was a solitary mastermind: Shane Rout. Operating in complete isolation, he wrote, performed, and recorded every single instrument and vocal line himself.
In the year 2000, he unleashed the project's defining masterpiece: the seminal album, Suicidal Emotions.

The journey begins with the haunting track, Depression.
Driven by a hypnotic, sorrowful guitar loop that repeats over a deliberate, slow beat, this piece became the blueprint for atmospheric despair. Listen to how the sorrow unfolds...

While much of the album is suffocating and slow, the second track, Betrayed, shifts into a frantic urgency.
It hits with a faster tempo, abrasive aggression, and a driving melancholic riff that captures pure, unadulterated anguish...

Then comes the return to Depression, marking the darkest emotional climax of the record.
Anchored by a heavy bassline, it gives way to Shane Rout's torturous, agonized vocals, laying bare the sheer desolation at the heart of the project...

For decades, Shane Rout remained an enigmatic and uncompromising figure in the underground.
Tragically, in March of 2026, the metal world lost Shane Rout at the age of 53, marking the final chapter for one of depressive black metal's true pioneers.
He channeled genuine torment into sound, leaving behind a haunting monument that will echo forever in the history of extreme music.
